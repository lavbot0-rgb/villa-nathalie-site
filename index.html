Welcome

A private sanctuary for slow mornings, golden sunsets, and effortless luxury.

Villa Nathalie is designed for guests who want privacy, comfort, and a refined sense of place. Whether you are planning a family holiday, a romantic retreat, or an intimate celebration, every corner of the villa is created to feel calm, beautiful, and deeply personal.

Villa Details

A beautiful luxury villa that can accommodate up to 6 persons.

Villa Nathalie is a brand‑new, tastefully furnished property with three bedrooms spread over two floors. It has a spacious living room with a fireplace, a dining area, air‑conditioning, and a kitchen plus a large entertaining area that adjoins the terrace and garden.

Located 10 km from the Franciscan Monastery, it offers a tranquil escape with easy access to local attractions.
